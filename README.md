### 1. Bank Profitability Diagnostics

**Python | FDIC REST API | Databricks | PySpark | Pandas**

Developed a financial analytics framework using quarterly FDIC banking data from 2022 Q1 to 2026 Q2.

- Analysed over 83,000 bank-quarter observations across approximately 4,900 banks.

### 2. Mortgage Refinancing Exposure Analytics

**Python | SQL | Power BI | Freddie Mac | PMMS**

Developed an end-to-end mortgage analytics project using Freddie Mac loan-level data and historical mortgage rates to assess refinancing exposure under different interest rate scenarios.

- Analysed approximately 9.65 million loan-month observations, focusing on 30-year fixed-rate mortgages.
- Evaluated an active portfolio of 211,265 loans with approximately $62.14 billion in outstanding unpaid principal balance (March 2026).
- Estimated current rate-sensitive exposure at 26.25% of outstanding UPB.
- Found that exposure could increase to 60.44% under a hypothetical 100-basis-point mortgage rate reduction.
- Developed Python and SQL analyses alongside interactive Power BI dashboards to communicate portfolio-level findings.

**[View Project](https://github.com/minhkhanh-trinh3196/mortgage-refinancing-exposure-portfolio)**

### 3. Australian Corporate Insolvency Analysis

**Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Databricks | ASIC**

Developed an end-to-end financial analytics project using Australian corporate insolvency data to identify industry distress signals and support credit risk monitoring.

- Analysed 58,115 insolvency appointment records across 64 reporting months (July 2021-October 2026).
- Reconciled monthly appointment counts against official ASIC statistics with no discrepancies.
- Identified historical and emerging distress signals across 19 classified industries.
- Investigated concentrated Construction appointments, demonstrating a shift in year-over-year growth from +12.89% to -5.14% under an illustrative sensitivity scenario.
- Developed an integrated industry monitoring framework combining historical and recent evidence to prioritise further investigation.

[View Project](https://github.com/minhkhanh-trinh3196/australian-corporate-insolvency-analysis)
